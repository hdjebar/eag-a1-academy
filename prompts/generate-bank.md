# Category rules

- abstract: use text-representable series, transformations, or matrices; avoid culturally dependent knowledge.
- verbal: the answer must follow from the passage alone; do not rely on outside knowledge.
- numeric: verify units, percentages, arithmetic, and rounding before writing distractors.
- planning: state every deadline, duration, availability, dependency, and priority required to solve the item.
- situational: use lawful, impartial, transparent service behavior; stay within competence and escalate appropriately. These are pedagogical principles, not an official scoring key.
