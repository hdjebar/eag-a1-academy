# Practice-question sources for the redesigned EAG A1 (Luxembourg, 15 Sept 2026): URLs by test category

The only freely accessible examples built for the post-15 September 2026 EAG are on psychotechnique.lu. It has one free worked mini-item for each A1 test type and a paid A1/A2 pack (44 €, reduced from 59 €, one-time payment, 6 months' access). For everything else you need analogous material. The best is EPSO's official reasoning samples, SHL Direct's French example pages, the Belgian travaillerpour.be-style pages on psychotechnique.be (especially the ++/-- rated SJT), official Canadian and Québec public-service SJT samples, and a few agenda/scheduling exercises in French and German. Planning (agenda) is the thinnest category. No source I found reproduces a true "manage an agenda" item except psychotechnique.lu's single mini-item.

## TL;DR
- **Only EAG-2026-specific free items:** psychotechnique.lu/examen-etat-luxembourg/ has a free 6-item mini-test. It covers verbal and numerical (true/false/indeterminate), abstract, agenda planning, precision and an SJT about a user with a missing document. Its A1/A2 pack is paid: "44 € 59 € −25%", "paiement unique, accès immédiat", with "6 mois d'accès" and "Jusqu'à 10 tentatives par test" (page "Mis à jour le 30 septembre 2026"). Treat its format claims as third-party inference, not official.
- **Best analogues per category:** EPSO official samples (abstract/verbal/numerical, FR available for verbal/numerical); SHL Direct FR (verbal V/F/?, numerical tables) and EN (inductive series); psychotechnique.be travaillerpour.be pages (verbal, and an SJT with a ++ to -- rating scale); Canada CSC SJT guide (5-point effectiveness scale); Bundesagentur für Arbeit (DE) and concours-formation.fr (FR) for scheduling with durations, deadlines and constraints.
- **Outdated/avoid:** ORSEU's 2025 EAG article, RoomSpace's EAG explainer and psychotechnique.lu's Police page still describe the old "bac à courrier" (e-tray) format. Useful for prioritisation ideas, wrong for the 2026 EAG. Third-party uploads of commercial EPSO books (Scribd, SlideShare, pdfcoffee, dokumen.pub) and a personal free.fr copy of a French test book are excluded as likely copyright-infringing.

## Key Findings

- **Official status.** GovJobs's FAQ on the 15.09.2026 reform lists A1 content as "Tests de raisonnement abstrait, verbal et numérique; Test de capacité de planification; Tests de jugement situationnel portant sur les compétences « servir le client‑usager » et « conseiller »". The same FAQ says "Des exemples de tests seront mis à disposition prochainement sur GovJobs".\[1\] psychotechnique.lu instead states: "GovJobs ne publie plus d'exemples d'entraînement accessibles avant l'EAG. En revanche, les consignes présentées au début des tests comprennent des exemples. GovJobs recommande aux candidats de prendre le temps de les résoudre avant de commencer". For reform context, the Chambre des Députés (chd.lu, May 2026) reported that Minister Serge Wilmes presented two bills under which "l'épreuve d'aptitude générale ne sera désormais valide qu'un an", with sessions organised "dès qu'un poste sera disponible… soit quasiment chaque semaine". **Recommendation:** re-check the GovJobs FAQ (https://govjobs.public.lu/fr/examen-concours/faq.html) periodically; a direct fetch on 30 Sept 2026 returned a 404, and official samples may still appear.
- **Format conflict on abstract reasoning.** Your GovJobs description says candidates complete series or matrices. psychotechnique.lu says each question shows nine figures and the candidate must find the single "intrus" (odd one out), explicitly "ni de compléter une suite".\[2\] Build both item types (series/matrix completion and odd-one-out) until official examples settle this.
- **Verbal/numerical format.** psychotechnique.lu describes information spread over several tabs or sheets, with each statement judged vrai/faux/indéterminé.\[2\] That is structurally close to Aon/cut-e "scales verbal" and "scales numerical" (T/F/cannot say from tabbed data).\[3\]\[4\] This makes Aon-style samples more relevant than classic 4-option comprehension items.
- **The pack is still in flux.** Its content differs between the page dated 30 Sept 2026, which lists "Contenu du pack (23)" (7 verbal, 2 numerical, 2 abstract, 4 planning simulations, 4+4 SJT), and an earlier psychotechnique.lu search snippet ("28 tests… Raisonnement verbal - EAG - 8 tests… Raisonnement abstrait - EAG - 6 tests"). The page itself says it is a "première version" to be refined from candidate feedback.

## Best 2–3 picks per category
- **Abstract:** (1) EPSO official abstract sample (via eu-careers AST page); (2) SHL Direct inductive reasoning (EN); (3) psychotechnique.be matrix/odd-one-out mini-test (FR), which covers both candidate formats.
- **Verbal:** (1) psychotechnique.lu Novaris item (EAG-specific tabbed V/F/I); (2) SHL Direct FR verbal (V/F/Ne peut pas savoir); (3) psychotechnique.be travaillerpour.be verbal mini-test. Add the Aon "scales verbal instruct" description for instruction-application items.\[4\]
- **Numerical:** (1) psychotechnique.lu Banque Altiora table item; (2) SHL Direct FR numerical; (3) EPSO FR numerical sample.
- **Planning:** (1) psychotechnique.lu agenda item, the only true EAG-style agenda item; (2) Bundesagentur für Arbeit Postkorb table with durations, deadlines and a model calendar;\[23\] (3) concours-formation.fr garage scheduling (constraint-based schedule building).\[24\]
- **SJT:** (1) psychotechnique.be travaillerpour.be SJT (FR, ++/-- rating per reaction, public service);\[34\] (2) psychotechnique.lu usager/missing-document item; (3) Canada CSC guide or PSC Test 375 for effectiveness-scale mechanics.\[47\] Use France Travail examples for "conseiller"-type user-advice scenarios.\[35\]

## Recommendations for your item bank
- **Model the response format on the official wording ("pertinence au regard du contexte").** Rate each response independently on a scale, as in the Belgian ++/-- and Canadian 5–6-point formats.\[34\]\[47\] Don't rely only on pick-best. Set scenarios at counters, on the phone and in email with users and citizens ("servir le client-usager"). For "conseiller", use scenarios where the agent must explain procedures and options neutrally.
- **Planning items:** combine fixed appointments, availability windows, durations, deadlines and task dependencies ("immédiatement après") in a half-day or one-week agenda grid. Ask "which plan respects all constraints" or "where can task X go". None of the public analogues does exactly this, so this is where original design matters most.
- **Verbal/numerical:** use multi-tab documents with V/F/Indéterminé statements. Include absolute-word traps, possibility vs certainty, and absolute vs relative change. Add a set of "apply the written procedure" items, which the official description mentions and most free sources skip.
- **Abstract:** write both odd-one-out-of-nine and series/matrix completion until GovJobs clarifies.

## Caveats
- **Verification.** I opened only the pages marked "Verified". For the others I checked the content through search snippets. Some fetches failed: SHL FR verbal returned 503, the canada.ca URLs were too long to fetch, and the Québec Cadre classe 3 PDF (https://www.carrieres.gouv.qc.ca/fileadmin/documents/examens_selection/exemples_questions/test_jugement_situationnel_cadre_classe_3.pdf) returned 404, so I left it out. Open each URL once before relying on it.
- **Reliability of prep sites.** psychotechnique.lu/.be/psychotechniquetest.fr (all TestsCareers), JobTestPrep, Open Exam Prep and similar sites are commercial. Their "EAG format" statements are marketing or inference, not official: the psychotechnique.lu EAG page displays "94% Taux de réussite" beside its packs while stating it "n'est ni affilié, ni associé, ni autorisé, ni approuvé par… organismes gouvernementaux". psychotechnique.lu says its items "ne sont pas des questions officielles de GovJobs".\[2\] None of the listed sites claims leaked EAG questions.
- **Copyright.** Several EPSO "sample test" PDFs circulate as third-party uploads (Scribd, SlideShare, pdfcoffee, dokumen.pub). There is also a personal free.fr copy of a French psychotechnical book. I excluded these as likely unauthorised copies. Use them, if at all, only for format reference.
- **Official examples may yet appear.** GovJobs's FAQ says examples "seront mis à disposition prochainement".\[1\] I could not open the GovJobs tests page directly, so I could not confirm its current state.
